This is a daily AI intelligence brief for senior AI practitioners and researchers.
An item is ON-TOPIC if it concerns artificial intelligence or machine learning in any
form, including: foundation models and research (architectures, training, benchmarks,
weights), agents and AI applications/products, open-source AI tooling and frameworks,
AI hardware and compute infrastructure (GPUs, data centers, accelerators), datasets and
data pipelines, and the surrounding industry, policy, and community discourse about AI.

An item is OFF-TOPIC if it has no substantive connection to AI/ML — for example a
personal life update, a non-AI hobby or product review, general politics, sports, or a
software/engineering post with no AI angle. When an item plausibly touches AI, treat it
as on-topic; only drop items that are clearly unrelated.
